🧵 Textile Calculator

Five small calculators for common textile-engineering quantities. Each section states the formula it uses, so the output can be checked by hand.

GSM Calculator

GSM = fabric weight (g) ÷ fabric area (m²)

Denier ↔ Tex Converter

denier = 9 × tex · tex = denier ÷ 9

Thread Count Calculator

thread count = warp density + weft density (threads/inch)

Fabric Cost Calculator

total cost = GSM × area × cost per gram

Yarn Count Converter

Nm = Ne × 1.693 · Ne = Nm ÷ 1.693
